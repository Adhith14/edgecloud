Remote Working Policy — Extract
Effective: 1 March 2026

Eligibility: All full-time employees who have completed their probation period
are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Part-time employees may
work remotely on a pro-rata basis agreed with their line manager.

Equipment: The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. Any
additional equipment requires written approval from the department head.

Security: Remote work must be performed over the company VPN. Confidential
client data must never be stored on personal devices. Lost or stolen equipment
must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

Availability: Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to
16:00 local time, and must attend the weekly team meeting in person unless
excused by their line manager.
