Quarterly Infrastructure Review — Q2 2026
Prepared by: Platform Engineering, NorthBridge Software Ltd.
Date: 8 July 2026

Overview
During Q2 2026, the platform served an average of 4.2 million requests per day,
up 18% from Q1. Total infrastructure spend for the quarter was £142,000, an
increase of 11% quarter-on-quarter, driven primarily by increased cloud compute
usage in the analytics pipeline.

Reliability
Overall availability for the quarter was 99.87%, slightly below our 99.9% target.
Two significant incidents occurred. On 14 May 2026, a misconfigured load balancer
rule caused elevated error rates for 47 minutes. On 23 June 2026, a database
failover took longer than expected (22 minutes), during which write operations
were degraded. Both incidents have completed post-mortems, and remediation
actions were assigned to the infrastructure team led by Sarah Okafor.

Cost Optimisation
The team identified that approximately 23% of compute spend occurs on
non-production environments left running outside working hours. An automated
shutdown policy is scheduled for rollout in August 2026 and is projected to
save £8,500 per quarter.

Outlook
Q3 priorities are: completing the automated shutdown rollout, reducing database
failover time below 5 minutes, and evaluating a move of the analytics pipeline
to reserved capacity, which early estimates suggest could reduce its cost by 30%.
