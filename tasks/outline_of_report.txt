Quarterly Infrastructure Review — Q2 2026
- Platform served an average of 4.2 million requests per day, up 18% from Q1.
Reliability
- Overall availability was 99.87%, slightly below the target of 99.9%.
- Two significant incidents occurred: misconfigured load balancer and database failover.
Cost Optimisation
- Approximately 23% of compute spend on non-production environments left running outside working hours.
- Automated shutdown policy scheduled for August 2026, projected to save £8,500 per quarter.
Outlook
- Priorities: complete automated shutdown rollout, reduce database failover time, and evaluate move to reserved capacity.
